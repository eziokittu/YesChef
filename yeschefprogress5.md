## Main implementation

### Trash, labels, and dialogue

The trash model is rotated 90 degrees and `TrashLidAnimator.openAngle` is negative, so the left edge rises from the right-side hinge. Trash particles are looping but remain stopped until the first delayed visit. `KitchenActivityEffects` starts a low-volume 3D fly loop at the same time and resets all three on a new match. `WorldLabelFader` now measures XZ distance, preventing elevated stove canvases from defeating proximity fading. Customer dialogue uses `CustomerSpeechBubble.png`, a single original outer silhouette generated by `Tools/Art/generate_ui_assets.py`; `DialogueBubbleAnimator` supplies the short bounce.

### Garden and marsh

The south garden has six irregular color patches and 15 clumps containing 120 taller wind-swaying blades. The marsh alternates three water materials and each facet now combines stronger phase-offset height, tilt, scale, and per-renderer color shimmer. Six standalone round leaves and a low-poly fallen log supplement the lotus plants.

### Audio

`Tools/Audio/generate_audio_assets.py` deterministically creates 18 original 44.1 kHz, 16-bit WAV files under `Assets/Audio/Generated`:

- `Music_CozyMorning.wav` and `Music_EveningCafe.wav`: two 32-second stereo piano/jazz pieces.
- Eight kitchen cues: chopping, six-second cooking sizzle, fridge open/close, prepared, new order, received item, and trash.
- Seven exterior layers: crickets, birds, wind, water, paddle/splash, frog, and sparse hiss.
- One quiet spatial fly loop for the trash.

`AudioDirector` owns the two-track persistent playlist and kitchen cues. `AmbientAudioZone` uses per-layer mix weights so water/wind form the bed while frogs, hiss, birds, and crickets remain accents. All audio respects the existing Music/SFX preferences.
